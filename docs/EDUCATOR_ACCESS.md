# Educator Access

Screen Record Cursor Educator Access provides a free personal-use copy to
active employees of public K–12 schools and public colleges.

## Version 1

Use the normal direct-download storefront and the exact same notarized app as
paid customers.

1. A short request form asks for:
   - Name
   - Public school or college
   - Work email or a public staff-directory link
   - One sentence about how the app will be used
2. Requests are reviewed manually.
3. Approved educators receive a unique 100%-off checkout code.
4. Checkout delivers the same app, updates, and support information as a paid
   purchase.

Terms should remain simple: personal use, non-transferable, best-effort support,
and no student data collected or processed by the app.

Do not build educator identity verification, a license server, a separate
binary, or in-app account activation for version 1. The app is open source and
the paid product is inexpensive; copy protection would create more support
burden than value.

## If an App Store edition becomes viable

- Use App Store promo codes for individual educator friends.
- Promo codes are limited and time-sensitive, so direct Educator Access remains
  the scalable free path.
- Use Apple School Manager education pricing for institution purchases when
  appropriate.
- Do not begin with a private Custom App; it creates organization-ID and future
  distribution constraints that are unnecessary for personal educator access.

## Success measures

- An approved educator can receive and install the app in under five minutes.
- Educators use the same support and update path as paid customers.
- No educator verification or licensing code exists in the Mac app.
- Free access creates authentic product feedback, not an obligation to provide
  institution-level support.
